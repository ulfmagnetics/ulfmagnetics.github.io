Hello! We are the Linguistics Club at Allderdice High School. We meet every Monday (Wednesday the first week) from 2:40 until 4:00, in Room 271.

What We Learn About

-> The scripts, writing systems, and alphabets of languages

-> Evolution of languages: how they change over time and borrow from one another

-> Different grammatical features found in languages

-> How culture and language are connected

-> Related fields like vexillology, geography, mythology, anthropology, and psychology

What We Do

-> Give presentations about linguistics & related topics

-> Conlanging project

-> Prepare for NACLO (Linguistics Olympiad)

-> Translator project (using HTML, Java, and Javascript)

-> Elicitation

-> Geoguessr

We compete in NACLO, also known as the Linguistics Olympiad, which is a linguistics contest for high-schoolers. Participants solve linguistics problems using logic and reasoning. Though it is helpful, knowledge of linguistics or another language is not necessary to compete.
